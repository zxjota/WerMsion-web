🧩 Welcome to WerMsion

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains in plain, friendly language how we collect, use, and protect your information. WerMsion follows a "minimal collection, maximum protection" approach, so you can use our app with peace of mind.

📦 What We Collect

  • Basic device info (like device model and OS version) to optimize display.
  • Usage logs (anonymous feature click statistics) to improve your experience.
  • Content you voluntarily submit (e.g., emails sent via our feedback feature).
  • We do NOT collect location, contacts, photos, or other sensitive data unless you explicitly grant permission.
💡 We never sell your personal information, and we never use it for ad tracking.

🎯 How We Use Your Info

All information is used solely for: providing core features, troubleshooting, security, and improving the product. WerMsion uses industry-standard encryption (like HTTPS/TLS) to protect data in transit.

🤝 Third-Party Sharing

We do not disclose your personal data to unrelated third parties. We may only share when: you give explicit consent, the law requires it, or it's necessary to protect WerMsion users' rights.

🔐 Storage & Security

Data is stored on servers that meet international security standards. We conduct regular security audits and use access controls, encrypted storage, and other technical measures. Despite these safeguards, no internet transmission is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

⚖️ Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can also withdraw consent or delete your account. To exercise any of these rights, email us at the address below, and we'll respond within 15 business days.

🧒 Children's Privacy

WerMsion is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. If we discover that we've collected information from a child without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

🔄 Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app or via email (if you've provided one). Continued use means you accept the updated terms.
